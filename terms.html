3. Payment

All ticket prices are stated in Singapore Dollars (SGD) and are inclusive of any applicable fees. We do not charge additional booking or processing fees.

We accept payment exclusively via PayNow, processed directly through our United Overseas Bank (UOB) corporate account. No third-party payment processors are involved.

To complete payment:

  • You will be presented with our UEN and the exact amount payable
  • Transfer via your bank's PayNow service using the UEN provided
  • Include your booking reference number in the transfer remarks

Payment must be completed in full before your QR code e-ticket is issued. Seat reservations without completed payment may be released after 24 hours.

Once payment is received and verified, your QR code e-ticket will be sent to your registered email address within 24 hours.

4. E-Tickets & Entry

Upon confirmed payment, a QR code e-ticket will be sent to your registered email address. Please ensure you:

  • Present your QR code (on phone or printed) at the event entrance for scanning
  • Arrive within the event admission window — late arrivals may not be admitted at the discretion of event staff
  • Keep your QR code confidential — each QR code is unique and can only be scanned once

Light and Shadow Media is not responsible for QR codes that are lost, stolen, or shared. Duplicate or fraudulent tickets will be denied entry.

If you do not receive your ticket email within 24 hours of payment, please contact us immediately.
